User: Get a list of categories where each product has at least one purchase. Sort ascending by id. Result schema: id | name.
ChatGPT: You can check if there are any products in a category that do not have any entries in the order_details table. If no such product exists, then all products in the category have at least one purchase.

SQL:
SELECT pc.id, pc.name
FROM product_category pc
WHERE EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM product p
    JOIN product_title pt ON p.product_title_id = pt.id
    WHERE pt.product_category_id = pc.id
)
AND NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM product p
    JOIN product_title pt ON p.product_title_id = pt.id
    WHERE pt.product_category_id = pc.id
    AND p.id NOT IN (SELECT product_id FROM order_details)
)
ORDER BY pc.id;
